BunnyMark

Click the canvas, then X +1 bunny, Z +100, C +500, V +1000 (on release), X+Z back to 0, M GPU / CPU drawing, S vsync (the display's refresh, or as fast as the browser allows). A gamepad works too.

A port of Timofffee's Playdate bunnymark, itself raylib's textures_bunnymark.
